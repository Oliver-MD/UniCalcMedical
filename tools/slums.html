SLUMS

Saint Louis University Mental Status exam — cognitive impairment screening. Clinician-administered; several items (clock drawing, shape recognition, story recall) require the examiner's own scoring per the official criteria.

Orientation (1 point each)

Calculation — "$100, buy a dozen apples for $3 and a tricycle for $20"

Animal naming (1 minute)

Backward digit span

Delayed recall of the 5 objects named earlier

Clock drawing (examiner-scored, per official criteria)

Shape recognition (examiner-scored)

Story recall (examiner-scored, out of 8)

Not a medical device. These calculators are reference implementations of published clinical scoring systems, intended for educational and informational use. They are not intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or patient care, and do not replace independent clinical judgement. The items above total 29 of the official 30-point scale — one point (within the language domain) could not be independently confirmed with confidence across sources and is deliberately left out rather than guessed at. Please verify the complete, current scoring criteria against the official SLU form before relying on this for any real assessment.
Reference: Tariq SH, Tumosa N, Chibnall JT, Perry MH 3rd, Morley JE. Comparison of the Saint Louis University mental status examination and the mini-mental state examination for detecting dementia and mild neurocognitive disorder. Am J Geriatr Psychiatry. 2006;14(11):900–910.
