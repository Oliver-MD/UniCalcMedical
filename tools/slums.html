Cognition & frailty

SLUMS

Saint Louis University Mental Status exam — cognitive impairment screening. Clinician-administered; several items (clock drawing, shape recognition, story recall) require the examiner's own scoring per the official criteria.

Education (needed for interpretation)

Orientation (1 point each)

Calculation — "$100, buy a dozen apples for $3 and a tricycle for $20"

Animal naming (1 minute)

Backward digit span (the practice item 87 is not scored)

Delayed recall of the 5 objects named earlier

Clock drawing (examiner-scored, per official criteria)

Shapes (1 point each)

Story recall (2 points per correct answer)

Not a medical device. These calculators are reference implementations of published clinical scoring systems, intended for educational and informational use. They are not intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or patient care, and do not replace independent clinical judgement.
Reference: Tariq SH, Tumosa N, Chibnall JT, Perry MH 3rd, Morley JE. Comparison of the Saint Louis University mental status examination and the mini-mental state examination for detecting dementia and mild neurocognitive disorder. Am J Geriatr Psychiatry. 2006;14(11):900–910.
