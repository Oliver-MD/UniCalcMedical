Opioid MME Conversion

Converts a daily oral opioid dose to its morphine milligram equivalent (MME), using CDC conversion factors.

Not a medical device. These calculators are reference implementations of published clinical scoring systems, intended for educational and informational use. They are not intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or patient care, and do not replace independent clinical judgement. MME conversion factors are intended for analytical/educational purposes, not as direct clinical dosing instructions. Actual opioid rotation must account for incomplete cross-tolerance (typically reducing the calculated equivalent by 25–50%) and always requires a licensed prescriber's judgement. Methadone and fentanyl are deliberately excluded here — both require non-linear, specialist-guided conversion and are not safely represented by a simple multiplication factor.
