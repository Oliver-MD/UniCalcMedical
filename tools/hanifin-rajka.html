Hanifin-Rajka Criteria

Diagnostic criteria for atopic dermatitis. Diagnosis requires at least 3 major and at least 3 minor criteria.

Minor criteria (need ≥3) — representative selection from the original 23-item list

Not a medical device. These calculators are reference implementations of published clinical scoring systems, intended for educational and informational use. They are not intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or patient care, and do not replace independent clinical judgement. The original Hanifin and Rajka criteria list 23 minor criteria; this page presents a representative selection rather than the complete original list. Consult the primary source for the full set.
Reference: Hanifin JM, Rajka G. Diagnostic features of atopic dermatitis. Acta Derm Venereol (Stockh). 1980;92(Suppl):44–47.
