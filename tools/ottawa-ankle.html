Ottawa Ankle & Foot Rules

Decision rule for when ankle and/or foot X-rays are indicated after an acute injury. The two are evaluated separately, since either or both zones may be involved.

Assessment

Not a medical device. These calculators are reference implementations of published clinical scoring systems, intended for educational and informational use. They are not intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or patient care, and do not replace independent clinical judgement.
Reference: Stiell IG, Greenberg GH, McKnight RD, et al. A study to develop clinical decision rules for the use of radiography in acute ankle injuries. Ann Emerg Med. 1992;21(4):384–390. (Foot component: Stiell IG, McKnight RD, Greenberg GH, et al. Implementation of the Ottawa ankle rules. JAMA. 1994;271(11):827–832.)
