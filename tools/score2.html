SCORE2 cardiovascular risk

Independent implementation of the published SCORE2, SCORE2-OP and SCORE2-Diabetes equations — estimates the 10-year risk of fatal and non-fatal cardiovascular disease.

Educational and reference tool. Reproduces published equations for teaching and reference. It is not a medical device, must not be used to make decisions about an individual patient, and gives no treatment recommendations. This is an independent implementation: it is not a product of, approved by, or affiliated with the European Society of Cardiology (ESC). Calculation runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent or stored.
Do not use for: established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease · familial hypercholesterolaemia · chronic kidney disease · pregnancy · type 1 diabetes · people under 40 years · (in type 2 diabetes) severe target-organ damage. These patients are already at high or very high risk, and this score would underestimate it.

Risk factors

Calibration region defined by national cardiovascular mortality (WHO). Default: high risk (e.g. Czech Republic).
Age 40–69: SCORE2 (or SCORE2-Diabetes). Age 70–89: SCORE2-OP.
If your laboratory reports mg/dL, divide by 38.67 to get mmol/L.
From age 70 the SCORE2-OP model is used, with diabetes as a yes/no factor — the diabetes-specific data below are not needed.
Diabetes-specific data (SCORE2-Diabetes)
IFCC units, not %. Conversion from %: mmol/mol = (% − 2.15) × 10.93.
CKD-EPI. You can calculate it with the eGFR tool. If the result is reported in mL/s/1.73 m², multiply by 60.
Not a medical device. These calculators are reference implementations of published clinical scoring systems, intended for educational and informational use. They are not intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or patient care, and do not replace independent clinical judgement.
